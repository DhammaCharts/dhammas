#### The sevenfold grouping of the noble disciples is as follows:

- (1) the faith-devotee (_saddhānusārī_),
- (2) the faith-liberated one (_saddhāvimutta_),
- (3) the body-witness (_kāya-sakkhī_),
- (4) the both-ways-liberated one (_ubhato-bhāga-vimutta_),
- (5) the Dhamma-devotee (_dhammānusārī_),
- (6) the vision-attainer (_ditthippatta_),
- (7) the wisdom-liberated one (_paññā_-_vimutta_).

Further details about the body-witness, the both-ways-liberated one and the wisdom-liberated one, s. under the three Pāli terms. Cf. also M. 70; A. IX, 44; S. XII, 70; Pts.M. II, p. 33, PTS.
