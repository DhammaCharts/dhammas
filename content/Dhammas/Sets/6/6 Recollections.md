1. Buddha
Bhagavā, 
arahant, 
rightly and fully awakened, 
accomplished in vijjā and [good] conduct, faring well, 
knowing the world, 
the unsurpassed leader of persons to be tamed,teacher of devas and humans, 
a Buddha, 
a Bhagavā
2. Dhamma
well declared by the Bhagavā: 
visible here and now, 
immediate, 
inviting to come and see, effective, 
to be individually ascertained by the wise
3. Sangha
practicing well,  
practicing straightforwardly, 
practicing rightly, 
practicing correctly,
the four pairs of men, the eight [types of] individuals, 
worthy of gifts, 
worthy of hospitality, 
worthy of offerings, 
worthy of respectful salutations, 
the unsurpassed field of merit for the world
