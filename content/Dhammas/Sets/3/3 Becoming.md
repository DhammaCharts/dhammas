3 forms of becoming: 

1. Sensual becoming 
2. Form becoming 
3. Formless becoming 

(from Samyutta Nikaya 12.2)
bhava
'becoming', 'process of existence', consists of 3 planes:
sensuous existence (kāma-bhava),
fine-material existence (rūpa-bhava),
immaterial existence (arūpa-bhava). Cf. loka.
