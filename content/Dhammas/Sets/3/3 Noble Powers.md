Noble power' _(ariyā-iddhi)_ is the power of controlling one's ideas in such a way that one may consider 
- something not repulsive as repulsive and 
- something repulsive as not repulsive, and 
- remain all the time imperturbable and full of equanimity.  
This training of mind is frequently mentioned in the Suttas (e.g. M.152, A.V.144), but only once the name of _ariyā-iddhi_ is applied to it (D.28). See further Pts.M., Iddhi-kathā, Vis.M. XII.
