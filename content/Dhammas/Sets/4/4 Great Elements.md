4 qualities of matter: 

1. Hardness 
2. Cohesion 
3. Heat 
4. Resistance to motion

1. Earth 
2. Water 
3. Fire 
4. Wind 

(Digha Nikaya 9)

dhātu
'elements', are the ultimate constituents of a whole.
(1) The 4 physical elements (dhātu or mahā-bhūta), popularly called:
earth, (pathavī-dhātu)
water, (āpo-dhātu)
fire, (tejo-dhātu)
wind, (vāyo-dhātu)
are to be understood as the primary qualities of matter.
