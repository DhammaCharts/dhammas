- yoni: 'modes of generation.' There are 4 generation from the egg, from the mother's womb, from moisture, and spontaneous rebirth _([opapātika](http://127.0.0.1:4040/ManualBuddhist/www.palikanon.com/english/wtb/n_r/opapaatika.html)_) in heaven, hell, etc. Explained in M.12.
