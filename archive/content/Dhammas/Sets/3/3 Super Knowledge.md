3 super knowledge: past life memory, seeing being been reborn, end of fermentations

Three Kinds of Supreme Knowledge (= wisdom). The three vijjā: 

1. reminiscence of past lives (pubbenivāsānussati-ñāṇa); 
2. knowledge of the decease and rebirth of beings (cutūpapāta-ñāṇa); 
3. knowledge of the destruction of the taints (āsavakkhaya-ñāṇa).
