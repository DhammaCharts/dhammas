4 Emotions (brahma-viharas) to be strived for: 

1. Loving-kindness (Metta) 
2. Compassion (Karuna) 
3. Altruistic joy (sympathetic joy with others‘ success) (Mudita) 
4. Equanimity (Upekkha) 

(from Anguttara Nikaya 3.65)
brahma-vihāra
