A V 136

Monks, if it is aided by five factors, right view has liberation of mind and liberation by wisdom as its fruit and reward. These five factors are:
Virtuous conduct (sīla; behaviour free from oppression).
Learning (suta: knowledge gained by listening, study, reading, etc.).
Discussion (sākacchā: conversing; debating; exchanging ideas; verifying one’s knowledge).
Calm (samatha: calming the mind; an absence of restlessness and distraction).
Insight (vipassanā: using wisdom in order discern things as they truly are).15 {702}
